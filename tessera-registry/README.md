## The problem

<Two or three sentences of business framing. No technology named.
 A reader who knows nothing about the domain should understand
 why someone would pay for this.>

A national ID authority needs a registry of persons, each with a unique and never-reassigned identifier, a small set of demographic attributes, and a complete, immutable record of every change ever made to their data. Auditability is not a feature here it is the product. When a citizen disputes their record, the authority must be able to show exactly what changed, when, and by whom.
